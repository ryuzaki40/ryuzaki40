### `01 / About`

Interested in understanding systems, investigating anomalies, and figuring out what happens beneath the surface.

My primary interests lie in **Cybersecurity, Security Operations, and Digital Forensics**. I enjoy exploring how systems behave, how attacks happen, how evidence is collected, and how incidents can be investigated.

Beyond security, I'm equally interested in low-level computing, hardware, operating systems, and experimenting with ideas that have nothing to do with my usual domain.

I believe in learning by doing, questioning assumptions, and understanding things from the ground up.

### `02 / Areas of Interest`

- **Security Operations (SOC)** — Security monitoring, log analysis, threat detection and incident response.
- **Digital Forensics** — Artifact analysis, evidence acquisition, investigation and OSINT.
- **Systems & Internals** — Linux, operating systems, computer architecture and memory.
- **Networking & Security** — Network protocols, traffic analysis and infrastructure security.
- **Technical Exploration** — Exploring unfamiliar technologies, researching ideas and building experimental projects.
